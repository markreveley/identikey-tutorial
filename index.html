Tutorial: IdentiKey v3

About this tutorial

IdentiKey is an identity and authentication service. It gives each person one identity, lets them prove they hold it, and hands the result of that proof to the app they are attempting to access.

This tutorial assumes no background. Each term is explained the first time it appears, in bold, and the Glossary at the end lists every term with the section that introduced it.

There are five parts:

Part 1 covers the three questions every login process must answer, and the cryptography underpinning them.

Part 2 covers how apps hand sign-in to a provider, and how IdentiKey does that job.

Part 3 describes IdentiKey's concept of an identity.

Part 4 covers custody: who holds the keys, and what IdentiKey does with the ones it holds.

Part 5 lays out the custody ladder, places the hosted service on it, and sets the stage for the rungs above and the proposed authority layer.

Contents and how to read this

Part 1 is a self-contained primer on the cryptography and on passkeys, and can be read on its own. A reader who already knows OAuth and OpenID Connect can skim Part 2 and go straight to Part 3, which is where IdentiKey stops being an ordinary identity provider. Parts 4 and 5 are the detailed account of what the hosted service holds and where it sits on the custody ladder.
